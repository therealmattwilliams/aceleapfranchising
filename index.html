Who Qualifies

Two things decide your eligibility

The E-2 is open to citizens of countries that hold an investment treaty with the United States. Eligibility is based on your citizenship, not where you currently live.

  • You are a citizen of a treaty country. The United Kingdom, Canada, Mexico, and over 80 others qualify.
  • You have investment capital available, typically starting around $100,000 for franchise entry.
  • You intend to actively own and direct the business, not invest passively.
